# Code of conduct

PuyuDB welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, or experience.
This policy applies to project discussions, contributions, and project spaces.

- Treat people respectfully. Critique ideas and code, not a person's worth.
- Be patient, explain disagreements, and accept corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, intimidate, or publish someone's private
  information. Sexual harassment and unwanted sexual attention are not welcome.
- Keep participation relevant and constructive. Do not use project spaces for
  spam or repeated personal attacks.
- Respect boundaries and requests to stop inappropriate behavior.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect
the community, considering the behavior and its impact. Moderation should be
proportionate and explained when doing so does not expose private information.

Use GitHub's reporting or blocking tools where appropriate. To request project
moderation, make a non-sensitive request for a contact; do not post personal or
sensitive details publicly. No dedicated private conduct-reporting contact or
guaranteed response time is currently published.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
